#### 这一版改了什么

**手机端「USB_ADB」这一行点了有回应了。** 上一版把它改成了点了什么也不做，是错的。现在的行为说明了它是怎么回事：数据线变成通道，是在电脑上跑 `adb forward` 完成的；手机本来就在监听同一个端口，所以这一端没有可开关的东西 —— 点它会这么告诉你，而不是没反应。

其余与 0.3.15 相同：深色模式的可读性修复、桌面端 USB-ADB 插线即连、未连接时远端版本显示 `—`。

#### 注意

- 桌面端插线连接：装好 platform-tools，`adb devices` 能看到设备，再在「连接方式」里点它。
- 手机端不需要开任何东西；保持共享开启即可。
- 两端请保持同一协议版本（v1）。

#### What changed

**The phone's `USB_ADB` row answers again.** The previous build made it do nothing when tapped, which was wrong. It now explains what it is: the cable becomes a channel when the desktop runs `adb forward`, and the phone is already listening on that same port, so there is nothing to switch on this end.

Everything else is 0.3.15: readable dark mode, USB-ADB connecting over the cable from the desktop, and `—` instead of `0` for a remote version that does not exist.

#### Notes

- Connecting over the cable on the desktop needs platform-tools on the PATH; `adb devices` should list the phone before 「连接方式」 shows it.
- Nothing needs switching on the phone — keep sharing on.
- Keep both ends on the same protocol version (v1).
